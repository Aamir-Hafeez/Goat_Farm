Build a premium, modern, full-stack Goat Breeding Cycle & Farm Management Web Application with intelligent breeding automation, livestock tracking, genealogy, QR identification, WhatsApp notifications, and advanced breeding analytics.

The platform should manage the complete lifecycle of every goat, from registration and breeding to pregnancy, delivery, offspring growth, and future generations.
